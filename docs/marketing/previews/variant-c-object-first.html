How It Hears

It knows the kick from the keys.

Three systems work together 125 times per second to understand what you're listening to — not just how loud it is.

Spectral Analysis

64 Goertzel bins decompose the audio spectrum into discrete frequency bands. K1 sees the sub-bass, the midrange, the treble shimmer — each mapped independently to colour, brightness, and motion.

64-bin Goertzel + 8 octave bands

Beat Tracking

Real-time BPM estimation with phase-locked bar tracking. K1 doesn't chase the beat — it anticipates it. Effects are synchronised to the groove, not to volume spikes.

BPM + phase + bar position

Onset Detection

Transient detection under 2 ms. The snare crack, the pluck of a string, the first consonant of a vocal — K1 catches the instant a new sound begins and triggers a visual response before you've consciously registered it.

< 2 ms onset response

Together, these systems are why K1 effects look musical — not reactive, not random, but genuinely connected to what the artist intended. 86 audio-reactive effects, each composed around the full spectral picture.
