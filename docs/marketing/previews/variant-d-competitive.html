Technical Proof

What 125 analyses per second
sounds like — in light.

K1 does not react to how loud the room is. It decomposes the full audio spectrum in real time, extracts musical structure, and maps it to motion. Every 8 milliseconds, the entire signal chain runs again.

Spectral Decomposition

64 Goertzel bins resolve the full audible range into perceptually weighted octave bands. Sub-bass through air. Not just "bass and treble."

Chromagram

12-note pitch-class energy extraction. K1 knows whether it is hearing a C or an F#. Colour follows harmony, not amplitude.

Beat Tracking

Phase-locked loop tempo estimation with onset-gated confirmation. K1 locks to the actual beat grid, not just transient spikes in loudness.

Onset Detection

Log spectral flux with adaptive thresholding. Detects the exact moment a note begins — not when it peaks, but when the spectral energy changes.

The Surface

330 mm of liquid light.

K1 hides 320 LEDs inside a dual edge-lit Light Guide Plate. Light enters from both edges and mixes inside the acrylic — so the output reads as a single, sourceless surface. No visible pixels. No hotspots. No banding.

Sourceless glow

No visible LEDs. Light appears to emanate from the material itself, not from discrete points.

True optical gradients

Colour mixing happens inside the acrylic, not through pixel blending. The result is smooth, continuous, and analogue in feel.

Third-colour mixing

Red from one edge and green from the other create a true yellow in the centre. Additive mixing inside the medium, not on a diffuser.

Centre-origin motion

All effects radiate from the optical centre outward. The dual-strip topology creates symmetrical, physical-feeling motion.
