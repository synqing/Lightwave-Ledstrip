Light, without pixels.

K1 does not use a screen. Its Light Guide Plate transforms 320 discrete LEDs into a continuous, pixel-free luminous surface. Light enters the edge of the acrylic panel and scatters uniformly through precision-etched microstructures. The result is not dots. It is not a grid. It is liquid colour — diffused, seamless, and impossibly smooth.

This is the technology used in premium edge-lit displays and architectural lighting panels, miniaturised into a 330 mm form factor designed for your desk, shelf, or wall.
