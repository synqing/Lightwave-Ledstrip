# K1 Positioning — Final Direction

## Banned Language (Permanent)

These are dead. Do not resurrect them in any context — landing page, press, social, internal docs:

- ~~visual instrument~~
- ~~optical music visualiser~~ (redundant — every music visualiser is optical)
- ~~ambient light object~~ (drains all power from the product)
- ~~more than decor / not just decor / beyond a lamp~~ (defensive)
- ~~instrument-grade~~ (pretentious taxonomy)
- Any phrase that sounds like it's explaining the product category rather than making you want it

## The Truth

K1 is devastating in both states:
- **When music plays** — it performs
- **When music stops** — it still dominates the room

That's it. The copy preserves this without apologising for either state.

## The Live Wire

**"Liquid light"** is the ownable phrase. It describes the LGP's resting-state behaviour with force, vividity, and memorability. It does real work. Use it as substance, not decoration.

## Ambient Section (Landing Page Ready)

### Section Headline

**Light that lives in the room.**

### Section Subhead

When silence returns, K1 shifts into 106 ambient visual programmes — seamless, organic luminance that makes the object worth having even when nothing is playing.

### Feature Callout 1 — The Surface

**Liquid Light**

The 330 mm Light Guide Plate diffuses 320 LEDs into a single, continuous surface. No visible dots. No harsh points. Light appears to flow across acrylic like pigment through water — breathing, shifting, settling into patterns that feel found rather than manufactured.

### Feature Callout 2 — The Programmes

**106 Visual Programmes**

Each programme is an authored composition of colour, movement, and pace. Some drift like slow weather. Others pulse with the quiet rhythm of a sleeping city. They are not screensavers. They are considered pieces of generative light design, evolving continuously so the surface never repeats the same moment twice.

### Feature Callout 3 — The Presence

**Always Present, Never Performing**

Most audio products go dormant between sessions. K1 earns its place on the shelf around the clock. In daylight it is a sculptural object. After dark it becomes the room's ambient heartbeat — present enough to notice, restrained enough to never demand attention.

### Photography Direction

K1 in a dimly lit residential setting — bedside table or low shelf. No competing screens or light sources. LGP mid-programme: slow warm gradient (deep amber shifting into soft white). Room visible but recessive: soft textiles, natural materials, a sense of evening. The image communicates permanence — this light belongs here, it wasn't just turned on. Shoot at f/1.8 or wider. If animation supported: 6-8 second loop of one slow colour transition — not a rapid showcase.

## FAQ Revisions

### Revise #1: "What exactly is the K1?"

"A music visualiser and liquid-light surface built around a 330 mm Light Guide Plate. When music plays, K1 renders 86 audio-reactive effects driven by full-spectrum analysis, beat tracking, and onset detection at 120 FPS. When the music stops, K1 runs 106 ambient programmes — slow colour morphs, breathing pulses, gradient flows — through the same optical surface. It earns its place in a premium environment in both states."

### Add new FAQ: "What does K1 do when no music is playing?"

"It becomes liquid light. 106 ambient programmes rendered through the Light Guide Plate — no visible LEDs, no pixel grid. Slow colour morphs, breathing pulses, gradient flows. K1 earns its shelf space around the clock, not just during listening sessions. Most owners have K1 powered on more hours in ambient mode than in music mode."
