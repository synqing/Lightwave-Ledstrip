## Common Pipeline Findings

- Required registered candidates render through the legacy unified `ctx.leds` surface unless the device is placed in an independent/zone mode. Source inspection found no `ctx.stripLeds`, `DUAL_CHANNEL`, or `roleFlags` use in the required candidate implementations.
- Pattern metadata tags such as `DUAL_STRIP` describe the visual idea. They are not the VP authored-surface contract. The actual direct-strip contract is `EffectRoleFlags::DUAL_CHANNEL`.
- For single-effect MIRROR baseline, the expected authored surface is `m_leds`, correction surface is `none` for candidates that skip colour correction, output surface is `physical_strips`, and expected mismatch is `false`. Runtime `vp stack` must still confirm each hardware sample.
- Most required candidates skip colour correction because they are LGP-sensitive, physics, mathematical, or advanced optical effects. That makes them useful for studying effect-owned optical behaviour, but risky as targets for a future global medium pass.
- Several source files consume `ctx.audio` directly while `PatternRegistry::isAudioReactive()` does not list the effect. That is a metadata gap for silence policy and medium eligibility, especially in the Time-Reversal/KdV group.
