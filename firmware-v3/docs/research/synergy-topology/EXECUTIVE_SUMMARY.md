## 4. Top-3 Surprises vs the GIVEN

The Context Preamble named LayerStack × FramebufferLPF × ControlBus reuse as the GIVEN multiplicative chain. Pass 4 adversarial review surfaced:

### Surprise 1 — The GIVEN multiplicative claim is conditional, not unqualified

INF-01 × INF-02 is multiplicative ONLY under per-layer-τ cutoff exposure. Under uniform global cutoff, the chain is ADDITIVE (orthogonal axes — composition + temporal smoothing — produce independent visual character without amplifying each other). ControlBus reuse is NOT zero-cost (~150–250 LOC of effect-side consumer refactoring). The "LPF × LayerStack × ControlBus ≫ additive" framing holds only with these qualifiers. **Pass 5 (if executed) should restate the GIVEN with per-layer-cutoff and effect-side-refactor qualifiers.**

### Surprise 2 — The strategic position rests on Phase 4, not Layers 0–2

The GIVEN is necessary but not sufficient for K1's competitive moat. Layers 0–2 (Foundation + Tempo + Memory) produce STRONG moats — software substrate any competent platform can replicate at the porting cost. The ABSOLUTE moats live in Phase 4 (Layer 3): T-01 (DualStrip × LGP × Bilateral Wave Interference) and T-05 (DualStrip × LayerStack × ParallaxDepthStack) require physical hardware competitors don't have. If Captain over-invests in V1.0 substrate hygiene at the cost of delaying Phase 4, the marketing claim ("Liquid Light. Music made visible.") outpaces the engineering deliverable.

### Surprise 3 — The cheapest critical-path move (INF-06 EffectRoleFlags) was a singleton lone-voice survivor

SA-3 (Composition + CrossLineage) was the ONLY subagent that surfaced effect-role-flags as a discrete primitive. None of the other 3 subagents proposed it; brainstorm-catalogue synthesis didn't elevate it to the 6 convergent themes. Yet without it, INF-02 cannot ship as a mandatory pass, and ~15 self-trailing effects would need retrofit opt-out hooks at V1.1. **The protocol's "watch divergent SSAs" instruction earned its keep here — singleton proposals do indeed deserve heightened attention. Pass 1 §7 + Pass 4 §3 T-05 document the saved cost (~150–300 LOC retrofit + risk of latent double-trail bugs).**
