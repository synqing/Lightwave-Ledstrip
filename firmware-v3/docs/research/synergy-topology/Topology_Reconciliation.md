## 1. Verdict

The two runs **converged on the structurally important conclusions** and **diverged on tactical sequencing and abstraction level**. Specifically:

- Both runs partially demoted the Pass-1 GIVEN (LayerStack × LPF × ControlBus is multiplicative only under per-layer-τ exposure).
- Both runs recommended strategic over greedy ordering.
- Both runs demoted multi-element Physics (Kuramoto / Pendulum / Boid) to V1.2+ on brand-voice fragmentation grounds.
- Both runs flagged LGP fringe-coherence and ESV11 adversarial-input robustness as empirical measurements gating launch claims.
- Both runs confirmed dual-strip × LGP × interference is the strongest moat axis.

The ACTUAL divergences are tactical. **Codex's ordering recommendations are stronger on three points** (PersistenceHelpers Phase 1, ProductFilter as graph node, dual-strip moat pulled to Phase 3). **Claude's contributions are stronger on two points** (external-domain candidates from adjacent fields; granular kill-list rehabilitation reasoning). The reconciled kill order in §5 takes Codex's structural framework with Claude's external-domain integrations.

**Captain action:** ratify §5 reconciled order; resolve §6 consolidated decisions in one planning meeting; commission empirical measurements before Phase 1 ships.

## 4. Divergence Matrix

Six divergences with explicit recommendations.

### Divergence 1 — Domain registry granularity

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domain count | 119 atomic across 10 prefixes | ~18 aggregated (D01–D18) | **Both useful, different layers.** Use Claude's bottom-up registry as the working catalogue (every unique primitive has an addressable ID); use Codex's aggregation as the strategic-discussion shorthand. Captain reads aggregated; engineers implement against atomic. |

**Rationale:** Claude's registry exposes domains like AUD-12 SpectralCentroid, AUD-13 SpectralFlatness as separate addressable IDs — useful when scoping a 12-LOC AudioActor extension. Codex's D13 "Audio Interpretation Extensions" treats them as a bundle — useful when discussing whether to ship interpretation features at all. Different abstraction layers.

### Divergence 2 — PersistenceHelpers placement

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase placement | INF-07 in Phase 4 (~250 LOC bundled with PSRAM ring) | D08 PersistenceHelpers as **Move 1.1 of Phase 1** (before LPF and LayerStack) | **CODEX WINS** |

**Rationale:** Codex's argument: "without shared dt-correct persistence primitives, global LPF risks becoming a one-off and physics work later will duplicate unstable math." Reviewing Claude's Phase 4 plan, the LPF that lands in Phase 1 would indeed duplicate dt-correct EMA math the Phase 4 PDE solvers also need. Better to extract the helper library upfront. **The dt-correct array LPF + emaArrayDt + dtDecay3 helpers (~80 LOC subset) should ship in Phase 1.** Heavier helpers (heatStep1D, velocityAniso1D) can defer to Phase 6 when their consumers land.

### Divergence 3 — D15 Product Filter as graph node vs meta-pillar

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treatment of brand-voice filter | Pillar G meta-filter applied as gate after engineering work | D15 Product Signature Filter is a **Phase 0 graph node** that prunes wrong branches BEFORE engineering work | **CODEX WINS** |

**Rationale:** Codex's framing is operationally cleaner. Treating the filter as a meta-pillar (Claude's framing) implies engineering builds the capability, then the filter rejects it post-hoc — wasted LOC on dead branches. Treating it as a Phase 0 graph node forces the filter check to happen during scoping. **Adopt Codex Phase 0 (Baseline Guardrails) as the reconciled entry phase** — a ~50 LOC tooling investment (kill-list documented as code; centre-origin audit pass over existing effects) that prevents accumulated brand-voice violations.

### Divergence 4 — Dual-Strip Moat phase placement

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cross-Strip Wave Interference | Inside Phase 3 V1.0 Heroes (mixed with F1/F2/F3/F5/F6) | Dedicated **Phase 3 Dual-Strip Moat** before Phase 4 Ambient State | **CODEX WINS (with qualifier)** |

**Rationale:** Codex's separation surfaces the substrate dependency clearly: Cross-Strip Interference + Reflective Twin contract enforcement + InterStripPhaseDelay infrastructure form a coherent unit that ships together and depends on Phase 1+2 substrate. Bundling them with ambient/wake-up effects (Claude's framing) obscures the substrate-vs-application boundary. **Adopt Codex's separation.** Qualifier: F4 Cross-Strip Interference must hold off the actual launch demo until LGP measurement (C-5) confirms fringe visibility; in the unlikely event measurement fails, F4 demotes to V1.1 and the V1.0 demo leans harder on F1/F2/F3/F6.

### Divergence 5 — Hub #3 ranking

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hub #3 | INF-04 TempoPhase | D09 Dual-Strip Phase / Interference | **CODEX WINS** |

**Rationale:** Codex's predicted divergence #1 was correct: "I may rank D09 higher than the other run — sources repeatedly state dual-strip is under-exploited and single-strip-impossible; I treat it as near-platform, not just an effect family." Re-reading the brainstorm catalogue:73 verdict — "the single most under-exploited K1 affordance" — Codex's ranking is faithful to source. Claude over-emphasised tempo phase as a fungible audio-side hub and under-emphasised the dual-strip-as-platform reading. **Reconciled hub list: HW-02 DualStrip / INF-01 LayerStack / INF-02 FramebufferLPF / INF-04 TempoPhase, in that order.** Promotes dual-strip to #1, retains LayerStack at #2, elevates LPF to #3 (Codex's #2), demotes TempoPhase to #4.

### Divergence 6 — External-domain candidates

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| External-field domains | 6 surfaced (Reaction-Diffusion, Phase-Conjugate Holography, Distance-Field, BeatEntropy, PerceptualJND, Kalman) | None surfaced — corpus-internal only | **CLAUDE WINS** |

| Aspect | Claude | Codex | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-Light treatment | Move in Phase 3 V1.0 alongside heroes (~100 LOC) | Strategic phase position (Codex's predicted divergence #5: "low engineering effort plus launch-video/product-ritual value makes it disproportionately important") | **CODEX WINS (modest weight)** |
