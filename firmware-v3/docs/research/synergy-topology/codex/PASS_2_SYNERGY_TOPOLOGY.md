# PASS 2 - SYNERGY TOPOLOGY

This pass consumes `PASS_1_DOMAIN_REGISTRY.md`. Purely additive interactions are excluded from the graph.

## SYNERGY TOPOLOGY

Platform Domains (hubs):

  [D04] LayerStack Overlap Composition - outbound multiplicative/combinatorial edges: 7 - amplifies: [D03, D05, D06, D09, D10, D13, D17]
  Rationale: the source synthesis says one ~150 LOC LayerStack unlocks 12 composition categories and V1.0 layered ambient/reactive composition. Evidence: brainstorm lines 75-83, 141; SSA-Composition lines 27-36.

  [D03] Global Framebuffer LPF / Softness Pass - outbound edges: 5 - amplifies: [D04, D05, D08, D14, D18]
  Rationale: ES used global LPF as universal trail and cross-fade, while K1 explicitly lacks it. Evidence: taxonomy lines 43, 90, 187, 213; brainstorm lines 35-40, 142, 154.

  [D05] ControlBus Render-Side Audio Reuse - outbound edges: 5 - amplifies: [D04, D08, D11, D12, D13]
  Rationale: five AudioDriver categories are pure render-side reuse and the broader leap is from audio reactivity to audio interpretation. Evidence: brainstorm lines 85-93; SSA-AudioDriver lines 203-219.

  [D06] Tempo Phase / Beat-Phase Exposure - outbound edges: 5 - amplifies: [D02, D04, D08, D09, D15]
  Rationale: tempo phase unlocks six-plus categories and closes a declared K1 lineage gap. Evidence: taxonomy lines 103-105, 141-144, 186, 212; brainstorm lines 42-52, 143, 152.

  [D09] Dual-Strip Phase / Interference / Parallax - outbound edges: 5 - amplifies: [D01, D04, D06, D10, D12]
  Rationale: the corpus identifies dual-strip as the most under-exploited K1 affordance and physically impossible on SB/ES single-strip devices. Evidence: brainstorm lines 65-73, 156, 161; SSA-K1Specific lines 24-45, 78-99, 250-272.

  [D01] Centre-Origin Radial Geometry - outbound edges: 4 - amplifies: [D02, D07, D09, D11]
  Rationale: not just a style constraint; it is the coordinate system that makes physics, bloom and dual-strip phase recognisably K1. Evidence: taxonomy lines 62-67, 157-183.

  [D10] PSRAM Frame History / Time Delay Ring - outbound edges: 4 - amplifies: [D02, D04, D09, D14]
  Rationale: arbitrary frame taps unlock echo, delayed twin, optical-flow and long-context time-warp. Evidence: brainstorm line 146; SSA-K1Specific lines 47-76, 206-228.

  [D15] Product Signature / Restraint Filter - outbound edges: 4 - amplifies/constrains: [D06, D11, D14, D16]
  Rationale: this is not a renderer primitive, but without it technical capabilities drift into rejected analyser/rainbow/developer-wow territory. Evidence: brainstorm lines 126-133, 159-161; SSA-ProductStrategy lines 108-137.

Capability Pillars (clusters):

  Pillar A: Infrastructure Combinatorics [D03, D04, D05]
  Internal synergy: the GIVEN layer. D03 gives softness and temporal cohesion, D04 gives overlapping composition, D05 gives low-cost audio axes. This cluster is independent enough to land before deeper physics or dual-strip work.
  Dual-strip implication: amplifies if LayerStack treats strips as layer roles, but base value exists without strip-specific effects.

  Pillar B: Musical Phase and Motion Memory [D06, D07, D12]
  Internal synergy: tempo phase plus dot memory plus pitch/chroma mapping yields centre-phase pendulum, motion-blur chromagram dots and pitch constellations.
  Dual-strip implication: strong when one strip carries current pitch/phase and the other carries mirror, echo or prediction.

  Pillar C: K1-Only Optical Geometry [D01, D09, D10]
  Internal synergy: centre-origin coordinate system plus physical dual-strip plus PSRAM delay makes Reflective Twin, phase parallax and inter-strip coherence.
  Dual-strip implication: absolute; several capabilities are geometrically impossible on single-strip hardware.

  Pillar D: Dynamical/LGP Motion [D08, D11, D03]
  Internal synergy: persistence helpers and continuum fields turn LGP diffusion into an intended output stage rather than a blur tolerance.
  Dual-strip implication: medium to strong for coupled fields; weaker for single-strip heat fields.

  Pillar E: Product-State Orchestration [D14, D15, D16]
  Internal synergy: long-context state plus product filter plus event choreography produces Liquid Stillness, First-Light and restrained launch identity.
  Dual-strip implication: every phase must preserve Reflective Twin so K1 reads as one object.

Bridge Domains:

  [D06] connects Pillar B <-> Pillar C - unlocks: phase-locked strobe lattice, dual-strip polyrhythm, tempo-beating interference.
  Why bridge: it turns dual-strip geometry from optical trick into musical proof-of-listening.

  [D04] connects Pillar A <-> every other pillar - unlocks: all layered versions, echo composer, context-aware blends, Tab5 overlay.
  Why bridge: it is the graph's highest-leverage architectural pattern.

  [D10] connects Pillar C <-> Pillar E - unlocks: delayed Reflective Twin, time-warp replay, long-context frame-history moods.
  Why bridge: it converts physical dual strips into temporal depth.

  [D07] connects Pillar B <-> Product Signature - unlocks: pitch constellations that feel polished rather than jittery.
  Why bridge: it makes intellectually legible music features visually premium.

  [D15] connects technical clusters <-> launch value - unlocks: kill-list enforcement and prevents high-complexity features from diluting identity.
  Why bridge: ProductStrategy's rejection of literal tempo-bank dots and scopes changes the graph's recommended path.

  [D17] connects external control <-> composition - unlocks: performance-turbulence layer if Captain wants controller-led demos.
  Why bridge: singleton proposal absent from synthesis; valuable only if LayerStack lands.

## HIDDEN LAYERS

Layer 0 (GIVEN): D03 Global Framebuffer LPF x D04 LayerStack x D05 ControlBus Reuse
  Domains involved: [D03, D04, D05]
  Emergent capability: mode catalogue transforms from individual effects into audio-driven overlapping compositions with uniform softness.
  Prerequisite layers: none.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes when strip-specific layers are allowed; otherwise moderate.
  Moat strength: weak-to-strong. Weak as software alone, strong when combined with K1's LGP and centre-origin product grammar.
  Evidence: brainstorm lines 35-40, 75-93, 141-142.

Layer 1: Reflective Twin / Dual-Strip Depth Layer
  Domains involved: [D01, D04, D09, D10]
  Emergent capability: strip B can become a phase-offset, delayed or role-separated twin of strip A while preserving centre origin and compositing with live layers.
  Prerequisite layers: D04 from Layer 0; D10 can land after basic dual-strip phase.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes - absolute. Single-strip competitors cannot reproduce physical two-emitter parallax or delayed twin.
  Moat strength: absolute for D09/D10 physical behaviours; strong for software expression.
  Evidence: brainstorm lines 65-73, 107-108, 146, 156; SSA-K1Specific lines 24-45.

Layer 2: Musical Phase Geometry
  Domains involved: [D01, D06, D07, D09, D12]
  Emergent capability: centre-phase pendulum, pitch constellations, beat-parity bloom and polyrhythmic strip interaction.
  Prerequisite layers: D06 single phase first; D07 for polished dot/pitch motion; D09 for dual-strip expression.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes - two mirrored pendulums or phase ratios on one LGP are not a single-strip effect.
  Moat strength: strong. Tempo phase alone is copyable; tempo phase across a dual-strip LGP is not.
  Evidence: taxonomy lines 141-147, 186, 189; brainstorm lines 42-52, 103-105, 145.

Layer 3: LGP-Native Dynamical Fields
  Domains involved: [D01, D03, D08, D11]
  Emergent capability: heat, wave, spring and fluid fields that look continuous because firmware dynamics and physical diffusion cooperate.
  Prerequisite layers: D08 helper toolkit; D03 for shared persistence; D01 for centre source.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes for coupled fields, but several heat/field modes still work on one strip.
  Moat strength: strong with LGP; weak on naked LEDs because smooth gradients reveal quantisation.
  Evidence: brainstorm lines 54-63; SSA-Physics lines 207-216; SSA-K1Specific lines 13-22, 101-129.

Layer 4: Context-Aware Liquid Stillness
  Domains involved: [D03, D04, D13, D14, D15]
  Emergent capability: K1 changes state between ambient, speech-safe, reactive and cinematic without becoming a random screensaver.
  Prerequisite layers: Layer 0; D13 classifiers or existing audio-confidence fields; D15 curation.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes structurally - stillness must maintain Reflective Twin so the product reads as one object.
  Moat strength: strong as product experience; weak as algorithm.
  Evidence: brainstorm lines 85-93, 106, 154-157, 159-161; SSA-ProductStrategy lines 43-54.

Layer 5: Frame-History Narrative / Time-Warp
  Domains involved: [D04, D06, D10, D14]
  Emergent capability: visuals can echo a beat ago, recall earlier phrases, or shift story arcs using real frame history rather than scalar memory.
  Prerequisite layers: D10 frame ring; D06 for beat-aligned taps; D04 for composition.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes when past and present are assigned to different strips.
  Moat strength: medium-to-strong. PSRAM is not unique by itself, but the dual-strip time-depth expression is K1-specific.
  Evidence: brainstorm line 146; SSA-K1Specific lines 47-76, 174-204.

Layer 6: External Performance Surface [singleton/divergent]
  Domains involved: [D04, D05, D17]
  Emergent capability: Tab5 physical gestures become spatial force layers on top of audio-reactive compositions.
  Prerequisite layers: D04 and K1 protocol validation.
  Dual-strip amplification: yes if knobs map to positions/strips.
  Moat strength: medium. Competitors can add controllers, but K1's AP-only Tab5 ecosystem and LGP turbulence mapping are specific.
  Evidence: SSA-K1Specific lines 132-152.

## Topological Reading

The graph has one dominant infrastructure root cluster ([D03, D04, D05]) and two strategic differentiators that should not be buried under generic engine work: [D09] dual-strip optical geometry and [D15] product signature filter. The most likely hidden-layer loss in the original GIVEN is that D09 is not a downstream "effect category"; it is a platform domain close to LayerStack in strategic weight because it changes which capabilities are physically defensible.
